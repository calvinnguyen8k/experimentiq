ExperimentIQ - AI-Powered Experiment Design and Validation Assistant
Week 4 Graded Assignment 4.2

ExperimentIQ helps Product Managers and Growth Analysts turn a synthetic A/B test idea into a traceable draft. The workflow extracts facts, proposes a design, calculates sample size in Python, and produces an independent AI risk review with a downloadable Markdown report. Facts, AI suggestions and calculated results remain distinct.

VERIFICATION EVIDENCE
https://github.com/calvinnguyen8k/experimentiq/actions/runs/45187570149
123 backend tests and 14 browser tests passed on the deployed code release. A fresh unauthenticated public workflow completed extraction, design, calculation, review and report download. The reference example returned 1,570 participants per arm, 3,140 total. The 20-case development evaluation completed 60 schema-validated stages; keyword-based risk checks do not establish human-reviewed methodological accuracy.
