# ExperimentIQ Submission Guide

Week 4 Graded Assignment 4.2 | 23 November 2026

ExperimentIQ is an AI-powered experiment-design and validation assistant for Product Managers and Growth Analysts. It turns a synthetic objective into source-labelled facts, a proposed design, a Python sample-size calculation and an exportable risk review.

## Documents included

Idea Validation Report: supporting desk research, actual owner feedback, technical evidence, a prospective user test and decision criteria. The official seven-step framework mapping and independent user-study results are not yet available.

PRD: one page, following the six components listed in the Week 4 Self-Learning Handbook: problem statement, target users, MVP features, out of scope, success metrics and open questions.

Specification and Architecture: the executable-contract specification, detailed architecture decisions and a concise system summary covering the AI modules, statistical boundary, operations and deployment.

Reflection: one page covering observed decisions, challenges and future improvements. Review the first-person wording before submitting it as your own reflection.

Demo: a two-minute captioned recording of the public app using synthetic inputs and real AI calls. Original fixture-based walkthrough remains separately labelled in the repository.
